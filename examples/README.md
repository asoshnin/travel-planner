## Shorter prompts, illustrative output

These show the shape of the output for smaller, single-purpose requests — not full builds.

> "Find me 3 hotel options in Lisbon for next weekend, mid-range, walkable to the center."

```

Illustrative only — a real run searches your confirmed booking sites live and returns three dated, real listings in this shape.
```

### `travel-itinerary-builder` on a short trip

> "Plan a weekend road trip from Munich to Salzburg, 2 nights, dog-friendly stops."

```
Day 1 — Munich → Chiemsee → Salzburg
Recommended departure: Munich by 9:00 AM, to clear the city before commuter traffic
builds. About 1 hr 45 total driving, with a 20-min buffer for the lake stop.

Break the drive at Chiemsee, Bavaria's largest lake — there's a dog-friendly lakeside
path around Prien that's an easy leg-stretch for both humans and the dog. Arrive
Salzburg by early evening; the Altstadt itself is pedestrian and stroller/leash-friendly,
but check your specific hotel's pet policy before booking (not all of Salzburg's old-town
hotels accept dogs, even when the city itself is very dog-tolerant).

Parking: Salzburg's old town is largely car-free — the Altstadt-Garage and
Mönchsberg-Garage are the two closest to the center (a real run names and links
whichever fits best, with current rates and a confirmed distance to your hotel).

Staying at: Salzburg · [Map](https://www.openstreetmap.org/search?query=Salzburg)
```

Illustrative only — a real run verifies the parking option and any named restaurant's opening hours via search, the same "verify, don't invent" rule shown in the full example above.
